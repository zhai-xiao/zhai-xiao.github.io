Xiao Zhai

PhD, Simulation Research Engineer @ Weta FX

I develop simulation technologies behind the invisible VFX, specializing in computational elastic solid mechanics and high-fidelity multi-physics simulation technologies for major film productions like Avatar: The Way of Water. As a Simulation R&D Tech Lead, I help create the physics tools that make virtual environments interact naturally with artist creations.

One of my key contributions was helping design, develop and maintain our multiphysics framework Loki, and integrate it into Weta's production pipeline. This unified system allows different simulation elements to work together, greatly expanding the creative possibilities for filmmakers.

I hold a PhD in physics-based simulation from Beihang University. Feel free to connect on LinkedIn.
